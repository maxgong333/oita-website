About Brooklyn Meditation

Meet Brooklyn
Meditation

A haven for Inner Peace in the Heart of Brooklyn

Brooklyn Meditation is a nonprofit meditation community offering a welcoming space where people can slow down, reconnect with themselves, and discover greater clarity and peace through guided meditation.

Our Story

Modern life moves quickly, and many people carry the weight of constant thoughts, stress, and everyday burdens without realizing it.

Brooklyn Meditation was created as a haven where people can step away from the noise of everyday life and rediscover the peace and clarity that already exist within them.

Our Mission

Helping People Discover Lasting Inner Peace

Many meditation practices help you feel calm in the moment. Our approach goes deeper — helping you understand and release the thoughts, patterns, and emotional burdens that create stress and limit your happiness.

Inspired by a Korean meditation tradition and thoughtfully adapted for modern life, our guided practice uses reflection and visualization to help you let go of the accumulated thoughts and beliefs that shape how you see yourself and the world.

The result is more than temporary relaxation. It is a journey toward greater clarity, freedom, and lasting inner peace.

How It Works

A Step-by-Step Path to Inner Clarity

Our meditation follows a guided process of self-reflection and letting go. Through four stages, practitioners gradually release accumulated thoughts and reconnect with a clearer, more peaceful state of mind.

The Method

A Different Approach to Meditation

Meditation can take many forms. While many practices help create moments of calm and relaxation, Brooklyn Meditation offers a guided process focused on deeper self-understanding and lasting inner change.

Many Meditation Practices Brooklyn Meditation Method
Guidance Practice independently on your own Guided practice with experienced instructors
Focus Focus on calming thoughts in the moment Focus on understanding and releasing the root causes of stress
Process Short sessions for relaxation, focus, or mindfulness A step-by-step process of self-reflection and letting go
Approach Often focused on managing thoughts Learn to observe, understand, and let go of limiting thoughts
Community Personal practice without a community Supportive community and ongoing guidance
Outcome Temporary moments of calm A path toward lasting clarity, peace, and freedom
Our Founder

Woo Myung

From an early age, Woo Myung earnestly sought answers to life's most fundamental questions: Why do people live? Where do we come from and where do we go? Why is there so much conflict in the world?

Through deep searching and self-reflection, he reached enlightenment and developed the meditation method now taught at centers around the world — including Brooklyn Meditation.

His teachings guide the core of our practice: helping people let go of their false mind and discover their true self.

Meet the Guides

Meet CJ & Elizabeth

Our guides are long-term practitioners who have been through the same process — and are here to walk alongside you.

CJ Lee

Guide

CJ Lee holds a Bachelor of Architecture and worked as an architect for a decade before dedicating her life to meditation and spiritual practice. After experiencing profound personal transformation through meditation—including significantly reducing stress and anxiety—she chose to help others discover greater inner peace.

With 20+ years of meditation practice and 15+ years as a full-time meditation guide, CJ has guided approximately 4,000 people and completed more than 500 hours of meditation training in the U.S., South Korea, Mexico, and Sweden.

CJ Lee is a meditation guide and co-director of Brooklyn Meditation who has led and collaborated on meditation workshops and initiatives with NYU, The New School, SolarOne, Berkeley Carroll School, Brooklyn College Academy, National Grid, and former NYC Mayor Eric Adams. During the pandemic, she received support from the Sara Blakely Foundation and has trained meditation guides serving communities across North America.

Elizabeth Cockrell

Guide

My meditation journey began in 2013, for a personal need to heal and settle down my mind. Finding peace through this practice completely transformed me, and I believe if it worked for me, it can work for anyone. I became a guide with Brooklyn Meditation when we first opened in 2015. I'm here to offer a listening ear and facilitate the self-reflection that helps you let go of what's weighing on you, and find your true nature — yourself who is at peace.
